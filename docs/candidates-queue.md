# Mining pass — Dhammapada (Müller 1881 trans.) — no mint this cycle

**Source:** Pali Dhammapada (verses 1-423), Friedrich Max Müller trans. 1881 (*Sacred Books of the East* Vol.10, Part 1). (local copy) (Standard Ebooks edition; US PD). Acquired 13 via github.com/justinstimatze/cupel.

**Verdict: no mint this cycle.** Survey identified three candidate primitives but each maps to either an existing elements atom or requires deeper distinctness work than this fast-cycle has time for.

## Candidates surveyed + verdicts

### Candidate D1: `thought-shapes-action-shapes-consequence` (vv. 1-2)

> "All that we are is the result of what we have thought: it is founded on our thoughts, it is made up of our thoughts. If a man speaks or acts with an evil thought, pain follows him, as the wheel follows the foot of the ox that draws the carriage."

**Verdict — DEFERRED.** The mind→action→consequence chain is the most-named primitive in virtue-ethics traditions (Stoic logos-as-judgment; Christian "as a man thinketh"; CBT cognitive-restructuring; Hindu karma). Elements/ has lex-bra5e Stoic chosen-response and lex-x6sbv dualist-reduction-as-cue covering adjacent terrain. A mint of this primitive would require careful distinctness-mapping against several existing atoms — better done in a dedicated pass with Stoic + Christian + Buddhist primaries surveyed together. Logged as future-work.

### Candidate D2: `cycle-broken-by-orthogonal-input` (vv. 3-5)

> "'He abused me, he beat me, he defeated me, he robbed me,'—in those who harbour such thoughts hatred will never cease. ... For hatred does not cease by hatred at any time: hatred ceases by love, this is an old rule."

**Verdict — DEFERRED.** The structural claim — a self-reinforcing cycle (hatred returning hatred) cannot be broken by more of the same; only orthogonal-input breaks it — is transferable and adjacent to lex-k2kdc constitutive-defense (where the defense produces the threat) and lex-spm8x feedback-loop-as-mechanism (the general feedback frame). Could be elements-novel as the specific *cycle-resolution* claim (the way OUT of a self-reinforcing cycle is orthogonal-input, not opposing-input within the cycle's dimension). But the distinctness work — against lex-k2kdc specifically, plus checking Aurelius-Stoic anti-anger passages, plus Tolstoy non-violence, plus King Jr. "darkness cannot drive out darkness" — needs more cycles than this fast-tick supports. Logged as future-work.

### Candidate D3: `appamāda (vigilance) as path to deathlessness` (Ch.2 vv. 21-32)

> "Earnestness is the path of immortality (Nirvāna), thoughtlessness the path of death."

**Verdict — DEFERRED.** Map to lex-jv983 fluency-trap (the inverse: the discipline of attention as defense against automaticity) and lex-wtkuz descriptive-vs-operational-metacognition. The "earnestness/heedfulness" frame in Buddhism is the discipline-half of the same family. Could be a sub-pattern but the existing atoms already cover the transferable shape. No mint.

## Why no-mint is the right call here

The Dhammapada is a verse collection where each verse is highly compressed and many verses re-state the same core teachings in different surfaces. Distinctness audit against the elements cannot be done verse-by-verse; it needs a thematic survey across the whole text (mind, hatred, vigilance, impermanence, non-self, dependent-origination), and each candidate primitive likely needs cross-attestation against Stoic + Christian + Hindu + CBT before minting. That work is real but not fast-cycle-shaped — better done in a dedicated mining session with multiple primaries surveyed together.

This is the same pattern as the Kimmerer survey below — deferred most candidates, returned to later with proper distinctness work. The fast-cycle mining-loop is calibrated for primitives that are unambiguously novel (Du Bois double-consciousness; Confucius rectification-of-names — both clearly absent from elements). The Dhammapada needs slower work.

## Logged for future work

- Wisdom-traditions cross-attestation pass: Pali Dhammapada + Confucian Analects + Christian wisdom + Stoic + Hindu Gita + Sufi — find the transferable cycle-resolution + mind-shapes-action primitives that survive multi-tradition distinctness audit

# Gandhi 1909 Hind Swaraj — survey (mint deferred)

**Source:** M.K. Gandhi, *Hind Swaraj or Indian Home Rule* (1909, public domain). ACQUIRED 2026-05-29 from mkgandhi.org/ebks/hind_swaraj.pdf → (local copy). Full PDF; text-searchable. 20 chapters in dialog form (Reader / Editor); Ch. 17 "Passive Resistance" is the canonical satyagraha statement.

## Transferable candidate atoms (deferred)

### Candidate A: `securing-rights-by-personal-suffering-as-political-instrument`

**Elements-claim:** The willingness to bear unjust suffering publicly (rather than inflict it) places moral pressure on the oppressor by making their use of force visibly disproportionate. The mechanism is not the suffering itself; it is the asymmetry of action — one side acts justly even under unjust treatment, exposing the moral position of the other side. This is satyagraha as Gandhi articulates it.

**Verbatim grounding (Hind Swaraj Ch. 17, p. ~37, (local copy)):** "Passive resistance is a method of securing rights by personal suffering; it is the reverse of resistance by arms."

**Cross-domain attestations:**
- Civil rights movement sit-ins (1960 Greensboro) — same operational mechanism
- Solidarność strikes Poland 1980s — bearing economic suffering for political ends
- Hunger strikes (Bobby Sands 1981; suffragettes) — extreme form of the same mechanism
- Vietnam Buddhist self-immolation (Thich Quang Duc 1963) — extreme limit case

### Candidate B: `soul-force-as-non-violent-instrument-class`

**Elements-claim:** Force-of-arms and force-of-truth/love-of-soul are two distinct classes of social-coercion mechanism. The latter is not the absence of force; it is a positive instrument with its own causal structure (moral pressure on opposing party + public legibility of asymmetry + capacity for indefinite continuation). Hind Swaraj names this distinction explicitly.

**Verbatim grounding (Ch. 17, (local copy)):** "The force implied in this may be described as love-force, soul-force or, more popularly but less accurately, passive resistance. This force is indestructible... The force of arms is powerless when matched against the force of love or the soul."

**Cross-domain attestations:** ~same as Candidate A, plus theoretical works by King 1958 *Stride Toward Freedom* + Nhat Hanh 1967 *Lotus in a Sea of Fire*.

- vs **lex-7gm7f** (via-negativa-as-development): lex-7gm7f is about reaching a target by subtraction. Satyagraha is about coercive-asymmetry; different shape.

# Pierson 2015 *Power and Path Dependence* (Mahoney-Thelen Ch.5) — no-mint mining-pass

Relative to K containing lex-mwgep, the marginal elements-information added by enumerating the 5 political-power-specific mechanisms is small. 3. **Existing molecule convention:** every existing `_tier: molecule` atom decomposes-into 2-3 existing atoms (e.g. lex-mmq8x → lex-rn9ht + lex-we98d; lex-3ydmv → lex-yg484 + lex-axa6h). lex-rax8z as drafted would decompose-into only [lex-mwgep] which doesn't fit the convention.

## Source authority

Paul Pierson, "Power and Path Dependence," Chapter 5 in *Advances in Comparative-Historical Analysis*, eds. James Mahoney and Kathleen Thelen (Cambridge University Press 2015), pp. 123-146. In the local corpus. In-copyright.

## What the chapter contributes (as elements-strengthening lineage on lex-mwgep)

Pierson synthesizes the community-power-debate (Bachrach-Baratz, Lukes, Gaventa on three dimensions of power) with his own path-dependence theory. The synthesis: political power exhibits the same positive-feedback dynamics as other path-dependent phenomena, but with a domain-specific elaboration via Moe 2005's authority-over-losers-with-no-exit asymmetry.

Pierson's iceberg metaphor (p.124): "Power is like an iceberg; at any moment in time most of it lies below the waterline, built into core institutional and organizational structures of societies." The metaphor names the EFFECT of lex-mwgep-style path-dependent power-accumulation: after sustained positive-feedback, the resulting power-asymmetries are no longer visible as open-contestation but as institutional-structure. Empirical research focused on observable-conflict systematically underestimates power-effects precisely because power has been INVESTED into structure.

The five sub-mechanisms Pierson enumerates (p.134) are all specifications of lex-mwgep's general positive-feedback mechanism applied to political power:

1. **Resource STOCKS transfer** — winners gain one-time spoils that improve their position for future rounds. Mechanism: standard positive-feedback (advantage → more advantage).
2. **Resource FLOWS transfer** — winners gain ongoing structural-rent-streams (the Monopoly game metaphor, p.134 fn3). Mechanism: increasing-returns-to-scale.
3. **SIGNALS about relative strength** — winners' reputation deters future challenges; losers' demoralization spreads. Mechanism: belief-formation feedback.
4. **DISCOURSE alteration** — winners shape the ideological frame in which subsequent debates occur (Lukes's third dimension). Mechanism: cognitive-frame lock-in. Adjacent to lex-85bw8 pretext-narrative-conceals-actual-function.
5. **PREFERENCE-CHANGING INVESTMENTS** — winners reshape constituencies via policies that create durable interest-groups invested in maintaining the new arrangement. Mechanism: policy-feedback / coalition-creation.

All five are specifications of lex-mwgep's positive-feedback within the political-power domain. None is an elements-primitive distinct from lex-mwgep.

The Moe 2005 contribution — that political authority creates non-voluntary impositions on losers with no exit option — IS elements-distinct. It's the recognition that PATH-DEPENDENCE IN POLITICS HAS A COERCIVE ASYMMETRY THAT MARKETS DO NOT. This could potentially be a future atom if Moe's full chapter is mined; for now it remains supporting-context.

## Elements decision

- **No new atom minted from Pierson 2015 Ch.5.**
- **No lineage edit to lex-mwgep either.** Pierson's 5 mechanisms substantially overlap with Arthur 1994's 4-mechanism enumeration already cited in lex-mwgep (Arthur set-up-costs ≈ Pierson stocks; Arthur learning+coordination ≈ Pierson flows; Arthur adaptive-expectations ≈ Pierson signals + preference-changing). The novel Pierson dimension is #4 discourse/ideational, which is already adjacently covered by lex-85bw8 pretext-narrative. Adding Pierson 2015 to lex-mwgep's lineage would be lineage-bloat rather than elements-strengthening.
- **Future-atom candidate flagged for future passes:** Moe 2005's authority-over-losers-with-no-exit asymmetry. Mining Moe 2005 directly could yield an elements-primitive on **coerced-asymmetric-exchange-distinguishes-political-from-market-coordination**. ## Follow-on: lex-dun6c minted from the same pass

A proposed expansion of lex-345ta turned out to contain a second, separable atom. lex-345ta names the TRADE (standing spent for admission) and is agnostic about how the pre-adoption looks; the expansion's claim is about the DISTORTION IN THE DISPLAY — public-channel bias and overshoot of the group's own baseline — which applies to insecure members already inside and trading nothing. Minted as `lex-dun6c insecure-standing-produces-public-overconformity-that-overshoots-the-groups-baseline`, attested across three independent literatures (Noel/Wann/Branscombe 1995 for audience-conditionality, Lakin/Chartrand/Arkin 2008 for automaticity, Labov's crossover for the cross-domain case). Reciprocal edges to lex-xj7s3, lex-7ra5h, lex-xu94g, lex-pn8qn, lex-r7zuy, lex-trady, lex-345ta.

The generalisation the mint makes, beyond any single source: the driver is **insecurity of standing**, not being outside the group — which is what lets one atom carry Merton's outsiders, Noel's peripheral insiders and Labov's second-highest status group without straining. Pairing it against lex-7ra5h countersignaling was the structural payoff: over-signalling and under-signalling are the two tails of one distribution over security-of-position.
